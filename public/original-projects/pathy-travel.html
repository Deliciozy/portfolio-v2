Problem Statement

To assist travelers in easily creating the travel plan, we offer a travel planning tool that helps them conveniently gather travel information, make related decisions, organize itineraries and compile the plan.

Detail Design

How to effortlessly organize travel itineraries?

After users finalize and confirm their selections, we would automatically generate the optimal travel route based on the locations, opening hours, etc of the chosen places.

How to enhance the efficiency of discussion within the group？

We allow trip planners to invite group members to view the list together and enable invitees to comment on each item, helping planners efficiently gather feedback.

How to edit or modify the finalized plan list easier?

We present the plan list in card format, allowing users to easily add, delete or copy content. And by long-pressing and dragging, users can easily swap the positions of different cards.

Design Solution

Some users would prefer to see an overview of the itinerary after the route generation.

Some users felt confused about how to edit and modify the plan list at first time.

Once the plan is generated, we first present the overview list to users, helping them quickly browse the content arrangement, and provide a tab-switching feature that allows users to switch to the detailed view.

Although we provided flexible itinerary editing and modification features by easy drag-and-drop or swipe, most users didn’t realize these features.

We offered a tutorial to guide first-time users through the convenient itinerary editing features.
